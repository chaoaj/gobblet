    How to play

    Goal: be the first to show 4 of your colour in a row: across, down, or diagonally. Sizes do not matter.

    Setup: each player has 12 Gobblets in 3 stacks of 4 nesting sizes, numbered 1 (small) to 4 (extra large). Only the top piece of a stack can be played, so stacks are used from the largest down.

    On your turn, do one thing

    1. Play a new piece from one of your stacks onto an empty square.
    2. Move one of your pieces on the board to any empty square. It does not need to be next to it.
    3. Move one of your pieces on the board on top of any smaller piece, yours or your opponent's. This is gobbling.

    Gobbling from a stack

    A new piece from a stack normally goes on an empty square. The exception: if your opponent already has 3 in a row, you may gobble one of those 3 pieces with a bigger piece from a stack.

    Important

    • You can move any of your pieces on the board, even one that is covering another piece.
    • If lifting your piece uncovers your opponent's piece and that shows 4 in a row for them, you lose, unless the piece you are moving lands on another square of that same line.
    • Touch-move: once you pick up one of your pieces on the board, you must play it. You can switch this off in New game.
    • If all 12 of your pieces are on the board, keep playing by moving them.

    Draws

    The game is a draw if the same position comes up 3 times (both players repeating the same moves), or if both players agree.

    Using this version

    • Tap or click a stack or one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square. Highlighted squares have a dashed ring.
    • Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the arrow keys to move between squares, and press Enter or Space to choose. While on the board or a stack, keys 1, 2 and 3 pick your stacks. Escape cancels a choice.
    • Red pieces have a white ring and yellow pieces are solid, so the colours can be told apart without relying on colour.
    • Squares are named by column letter (A to D, left to right) and row number (1 to 4, top to bottom).
